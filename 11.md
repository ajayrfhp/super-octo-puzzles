# Transformation of Random Variables: The CDF Method

The **CDF Method** (or Method of Distribution Functions) is a fundamental approach for finding the PDF of a function of random variables $Y = g(X)$ or $Z = g(X, Y)$.

Unlike the Jacobian transformation formula, this method relies on first finding the Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF) by integrating over a region, and then differentiating to find the PDF.

## The General Procedure

1.  **Set up the CDF definition:**
    $$F_Z(z) = P(Z \le z) = P(g(X, Y) \le z)$$
2.  **Identify the Region:**
    Determine the region $\mathcal{R}_z$ in the $(x, y)$ plane where the inequality $g(x, y) \le z$ holds.
3.  **Integrate the Joint PDF:**
    Compute the probability by integrating the joint PDF $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$ over $\mathcal{R}_z$:
    $$F_Z(z) = \iint_{\mathcal{R}_z} f_{X,Y}(x,y) \, dx \, dy$$
4.  **Differentiate:**
    Obtain the PDF by taking the derivative of the CDF:
    $$f_Z(z) = \frac{d}{dz} F_Z(z)$$

---

## Example 1: Simple Linear Transform (1D)

**Problem:** Let $X \sim \text{Exponential}(1)$. Find the PDF of $Y = 3X$.
* **PDF of X:** $f_X(x) = e^{-x}$ for $x \ge 0$.

**Step 1: Set up CDF**
$$F_Y(y) = P(Y \le y) = P(3X \le y)$$

**Step 2: Isolate X**
$$P(X \le \frac{y}{3})$$

**Step 3: Integrate $f_X(x)$**
$$F_Y(y) = \int_{0}^{y/3} e^{-x} \, dx$$
$$= \left[ -e^{-x} \right]_0^{y/3} = 1 - e^{-y/3}$$

**Step 4: Differentiate**
$$f_Y(y) = \frac{d}{dy} (1 - e^{-y/3}) = \frac{1}{3}e^{-y/3}$$

*Result: $Y \sim \text{Exponential}(1/3)$*

## Example 2: Complex 2D Transform (Sum of RVs)

**Problem:** Let $X, Y$ be independent Uniform$(0,1)$ variables. Find the PDF of $Z = X + Y$.
* **Joint PDF:** $f_{X,Y}(x,y) = 1$ for $0 \le x,y \le 1$.

**Step 1: Set up CDF**
$$F_Z(z) = P(X + Y \le z)$$
This requires integrating the area under the line $y = z - x$ within the unit square.

**Step 2: Area Integration (Two Cases)**

* **Case 1: $0 \le z \le 1$** (Triangle in lower left)
    The region is a triangle with base $z$ and height $z$.
    $$F_Z(z) = \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \text{base} \cdot \text{height} = \frac{1}{2}z^2$$

* **Case 2: $1 < z \le 2$** (Unit square minus upper right triangle)
    Instead of integrating the complex shape, take Total Area ($1$) minus the small triangle at the top right corner.
    The top right triangle has side lengths $(2-z)$.
    $$F_Z(z) = 1 - \frac{1}{2}(2-z)^2$$

**Step 3: Differentiate to find PDF**

* **For $0 \le z \le 1$:**
    $$f_Z(z) = \frac{d}{dz}(\frac{1}{2}z^2) = z$$

* **For $1 < z \le 2$:**
    $$f_Z(z) = \frac{d}{dz}(1 - \frac{1}{2}(2-z)^2) = - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2(2-z) \cdot (-1) = 2 - z$$

**Final PDF:**
$$f_Z(z) = \begin{cases} z & 0 \le z \le 1 \\ 2-z & 1 < z \le 2 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
*(This is the Triangular Distribution)*
